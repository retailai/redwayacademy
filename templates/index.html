Our story

Red = the teacher's red pen mark, identifying a mistake. Way = the path. So Redway = the path of learning by identifying mistakes. A student who understands and corrects a mistake remembers it for much longer. We do not say "fail" — we say: ভুল করো, বোঝো, সঠিক করো.

The Red Pen Method

1. Red Pen Review

After every test, mistakes are marked in red and discussed in class.

2. ভুলের খাতা — Mistake Notebook

Every student keeps their own notebook: the mistake, its cause, and the correct solution.

3. Weekly Retry Test

A short re-test on last week's wrong questions only.

4. Red to Green

When fixed, a green tick next to the red mark. Monthly Green Progress report to parents.
